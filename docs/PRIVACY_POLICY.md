## 1. Anonymity — what it does and does not mean
The bot hides your Telegram username, phone number and Telegram ID from other users. This anonymity
applies **between users only**. Your messages are relayed through the bot's servers and Telegram's
infrastructure, so the operator and Telegram can technically access them. Do not share secrets.

## 2. What we store
| Data | Why | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram ID | identify your account | until deletion |
| Profile: name, age, optional country, gender | profile management | until you change/delete it |
| Terms acceptance time, registration date, last activity | service operation, abuse prevention | until deletion |
| Membership, subscription and payment records (Stars charge IDs, amounts) | Premium, refunds, accounting | as required by law |
| Daily search counters | enforce daily limits | until deletion |
| Block list, reports you file or that concern you, bans | safety | until deletion |
| Message **metadata** (type, time, ids) | moderation evidence | [7] days, then deleted automatically |

We do **not** store the text or media of your messages in our database.

Profile details are not shown to chat partners.

## 3. Reports
When you report a partner, we save the category, your optional description and the ids of the last
messages you received from them. Administrators may display those messages directly from your own
Telegram chat. If you deleted them, they cannot be shown.

## 4. Sharing
We do not sell data. Data is processed by Telegram (messaging, payments) and our hosting/database
providers ([RAILWAY / MONGODB HOST]). We may disclose data if legally required.

## 5. Your rights
Contact us to access, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account removes profile, usage and
block data; payment records may be kept where the law requires.

## 6. Children
The service is only for people who meet the minimum age stated in the Terms. Accounts of suspected
underage users are removed.

## 7. Changes
We will announce material changes in the bot.
